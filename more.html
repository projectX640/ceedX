Savings Accounts

Saving with QK Traders SACCO gives you a practical place to build financial security at your own pace. Whether you are planning for school fees, a business goal, an emergency fund, or a major purchase, a regular saving habit helps turn a future plan into something achievable.

Choose a savings option that fits your goal

  • Regular savings help you set money aside consistently while keeping your financial goals in view.
  • Fixed deposits are suited to funds you are comfortable committing for an agreed period while you work towards a longer-term target.
  • Junior savings give families a simple way to introduce young people to the value of saving.
  • Group savings support VSLAs, community groups, and co-operatives that are growing together.

Our team can guide you through account opening, saving plans, statements, and the current terms that apply to your chosen account.

Loan Products

When a meaningful opportunity or urgent need arises, the right credit can help you move forward without losing momentum. QK Traders SACCO offers loan options designed around the different reasons members borrow, with guidance to help you choose a repayment plan you can manage responsibly.

Credit for the moments that matter

  • Personal loans can support planned household needs and important life goals.
  • Business loans help eligible members invest in stock, equipment, working capital, or growth.
  • Asset financing can help with eligible vehicles, machinery, and tools that support your work.
  • Emergency and education loans provide options when family needs or school costs need timely attention.

Before applying, our team will explain eligibility, required documents, repayment expectations, collateral requirements, and the terms for the product you select.

Digital Banking

Your SACCO should fit into your day. Our digital banking channels are designed to make it easier to stay informed about your money, access supported services, and keep track of your relationship with QK Traders SACCO without unnecessary trips.

Convenience with peace of mind

  • Mobile banking gives eligible members access to supported transfers, balance checks, and loan enquiries.
  • USSD access makes selected services available from a phone, even when mobile data is not available.
  • Online account access can help you follow available savings and loan information in one place.
  • Alerts and statements keep you updated on account activity through supported channels.

Protect your account by using official QK SACCO channels only. Never share your PIN, password, or verification code with anyone.
